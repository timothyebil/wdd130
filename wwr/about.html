White Water Rafting

Our core mission at Splashdown Rafting is to provide safe, exhilarating, and completely unforgettable river excursions. We combine high-quality outdoor adventure with strict eco-tourism principles, promoting local waterway conservation and deep community camaraderie on every single journey we guide down the white water rapids.

History

Splashdown Rafting was founded over fifteen years ago by a small crew of passionate river guides who wanted to share the raw beauty of untamed river canyons with the world. Starting with only one second-hand rubber raft and a single rugged transport truck, our team focused heavily on providing unmatched personal safety and deep local knowledge of natural river systems.

Over the past decade, we have expanded our fleet to include top-tier safety gear, specialized self-bailing rafts, and comprehensive swift-water rescue training for every lead guide. Today, we are proud to be recognized as a premier wilderness outfitter, having safely introduced thousands of happy families, corporate teams, and thrill-seekers to the wonders of backcountry river running.
