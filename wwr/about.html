White Water Rafting

Welcome to Splashdown Rafting Company, engineered by Timothy Ebil. Our core mission is to provide safe, exhilarating, and completely unforgettable river excursions. We combine high-quality outdoor adventure with strict architectural safety design principles, promoting local waterway conservation, sustainable regional investment pathways, and deep community camaraderie on every single journey we guide down the white water rapids.

History

Splashdown Rafting was founded over fifteen years ago by a crew of passionate engineering and river systems integration experts who wanted to map out and share the raw beauty of untamed river canyons with the world. Starting with only one second-hand rubber raft, structural planning blueprints, and a single rugged transport truck, our team focused heavily on building robust safety infrastructures and an accessible operational design for clean water running.

Over the past decade, we scaled our regional footprints by integrating state-of-the-art communication frameworks, specialized self-bailing rafts, and swift-water rescue educational seminars for every lead guide. Today, we leverage modern project management methods to manage premier wilderness expeditions, having safely introduced thousands of families, student groups, and tech teams to the wonders of backcountry river running.
