Splashdown Rafting Company

Our mission is to provide safe, exhilarating, and unforgettable white water rafting adventures while promoting river conservation and deep team camaraderie on every single journey down the water.

History

Founded by passionate river guides, our company started small with a single rubber raft and a dream to share the untamed beauty of the river. Over the past decade, we have grown into a premier adventure team, guiding thousands of happy visitors through legendary rapids with safety and expertise.
