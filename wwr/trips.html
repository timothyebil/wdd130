Our Signature River Excursions

From peaceful family backcountry drift tours to high-velocity Class V rapid descents, our curated wilderness packages are built to challenge your boundaries while prioritizing absolute physical safety metrics.

Los Dientes del Diablo Run

Designed strictly for seasoned thrill-seekers. This high-velocity journey forces your paddle squad through legendary, narrow Class V rock drops and fast hydraulic water walls. Includes advanced tactical rapid maneuvering tutorials and full technical equipment configurations.

Duration: Full Day (6 Hours) • Difficulty: Class V (Extreme)

Big Mallard Canyon Descent

Our most widely requested intermediate excursion. Perfect for adventurous families and corporate team-building events. Experience a highly satisfying combination of rolling wave trains and technical class III drops, interspersed with clean, scenic drift sections beneath sandstone walls.

Duration: Half Day (4 Hours) • Difficulty: Class III-IV (Intermediate)

The Family Emerald Drift

A relaxing, entry-level drift tour designed specifically for multi-generational families with young children or elderly relatives. Soak in local geographic histories, swim in gentle natural river pools, and spot regional bald eagles nesting along calm, slow-moving emerald water corridors.

Duration: 2.5 Hours • Difficulty: Class I-II (Relaxed)
